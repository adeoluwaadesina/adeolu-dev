# Archery — listing copy

## Short description (≤120 chars)
Beat the wind, pull the perfect draw, and climb the archery ladder from Beginner to Expert.

(96 characters)

## Long description
A simple, skill-first archery range. Wind pushes your arrow sideways — read the
indicator and drag to compensate. How long you hold your draw determines your
elevation, so timing the release matters as much as aim. Three arrows a round,
five tiers from Beginner to Expert, each one harder and farther than the last.

Climb the ladder at your own pace, chase your best round on the leaderboard, and
dress up your kit with a gold bow or a flame arrow trail if you want to show off.
No energy timers, no pay-to-win — Expert can be earned by climbing, or unlocked
outright if you'd rather skip straight to the hard stuff.
